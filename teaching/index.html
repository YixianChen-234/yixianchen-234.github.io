Teaching

Teaching, supervision, and mentoring.

Supervision

  • 2019 – 2024 — Co-supervised a PhD student at the Institute of Soil and Water Conservation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, on gully erosion modelling with machine learning and gully classification on the Tibetan Plateau. Now an Assistant Professor at Shanxi Agricultural University.
  • 2023 – 2026 — Co-supervised an MSc student at Northwest University on gully occurrence and morphology modelling in the Northeastern Black Soil region of China.
  • 2025 – present — Supervising five undergraduate researchers at Northwest University on the effect of hillslope gullies on hydrological connectivity and downstream soil erosion.
  • 2026 — Co-led graduate students assessing the impact of dynamic river slope on flood inundation mapping at the CUAHSI Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute.

Teaching
