**Role:** PI · **Funder:** China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, General Project · **Award:** ~$11,000 USD · **Period:** 2021 – 2023

Gully volume is the quantity that matters for sediment budgets, but it is the hardest gully property to measure directly at scale — field survey of individual gullies does not extend to regions, and planar measurements from imagery capture area and length rather than depth.

This project examined the morphological characteristics of gullies on the Chinese Loess Plateau and developed models that estimate gully volume from more readily measured morphological properties, so that regional-scale gully erosion assessment can proceed from remote sensing and terrain data rather than exhaustive field survey.

Related work from this period established the scaling relationships between gully area, length and volume, and the accuracy with which gully planform can be interpreted from high-resolution satellite imagery.
